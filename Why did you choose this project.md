I chose this project because I wanted to understand how modern vulnerability intelligence platforms are engineered from scratch.



During my cybersecurity learning journey, especially ***while solving TryHackMe labs*** related to vulnerability assessment and CVE research, I noticed that ***analysts often refer to multiple sources*** such as MITRE, NVD, EPSS, CISA KEV, ExploitDB, and GitHub to gather complete information about a vulnerability. That made me ***curious*** about how these different sources are integrated and continuously maintained behind the scenes.



My objective was not to build a replacement for existing commercial platforms. Instead, I ***wanted to understand and implement*** the engineering required to build a ***self-hosted vulnerability intelligence platform***—from data collection and normalization to enrichment, indexing, deployment, and continuous synchronization.



I also ***wanted to apply the knowledge*** I had gained throughout my learning journey, including Python development, networking, Linux, cybersecurity concepts, Docker, OpenSearch, CI/CD, Terraform, AWS, and automation, by ***integrating them into a single end-to-end system*** instead of learning each technology in separately.



As a project evolved, I extended it with SBOM-based software risk assessment so that organizations can correlate their software inventory with vulnerability intelligence and generate a prioritized assessment reports.



Through this project, I ***gained experience in designing, building, deploying, and operating a production-style backend system***.
